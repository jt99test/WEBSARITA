## Notes for the optimization pass

1. **Highest-leverage slots**: homepage hero lead, homepage formación teaser H2,
   service-page hero subs, and the two formación cross-sell bands. These carry the
   persuasion load.
2. **Do NOT change**: the SEO H2s ("¿Qué es una carta natal…?") — they exist for
   search intent, not persuasion. Same for the H1s, which carry the target keywords
   (carta natal Barcelona, sinastría, etc.). Rework wording around the keyword, keep
   the keyword.
3. **Candidate direction discussed**: outcome/identity questions over topic labels.
   E.g. homepage formación teaser "Profundiza en la Astrología Psicológica" →
   something like "¿Quieres que la lean por ti… o aprender a leerla tú?" — but keep
   question-headlines the exception, not the rule, or the page reads like a funnel.
4. Every CTA goes to WhatsApp with a pre-filled per-context message; "first
   orientation free" is the risk-reversal and appears in the notice bar, hero note,
   and strip — avoid adding it to more places or it cheapens.
